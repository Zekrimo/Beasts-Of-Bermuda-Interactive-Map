About this Beasts of Bermuda map

Beasts of Bermuda map guide

Beasts of Bermuda is a multiplayer dinosaur survival game set across the islands of Bermuda. Use this interactive map to plan routes, find important landmarks, and explore the game world.

The map includes land shrine and water shrine locations, linked portal locations, tunnel routes, underwater areas, regional boundaries, and event zones. Use the controls to switch map views or show only the waypoint categories you need.

Hover over a shrine or portal for its name. Hovering over a portal also shows the location of its linked destination.
